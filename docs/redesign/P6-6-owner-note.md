# The new Murzak POS: what moved

This page is for the shop owner and the people who work in the shop. Print it or share it. Nothing has been removed; some things
have new names or a new place. The New look is on for every shop. If an owner switches it off in Settings, the shop goes back to the
earlier screens at once.

## The menu

The menu on the left now has Home, then four groups, and Settings at the bottom. The till is the button at the top.

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| Home | A summary of today: sales, profit, what is owed, and what needs attention |
| Sell | Sales, Customers |
| Stock | Products, Stock levels |
| Buy | Purchases, Suppliers |
| Money | Expenses, Reports |
| Settings (bottom) | Business, Till, Tax, Team, Advanced |

## Words that changed

| You used to see | You now see |
|---|---|
| Open POS, Point of Sale | Go to till, Till |
| New Sale (POS) | New sale |
| GRN, Create GRN | Receive goods |
| GRN List | Goods received |
| Submit Order | Send order to supplier |
| Material Receipts | Stock in |
| Material Issues | Stock out or write-off |
| Reconciliation | Count stock |
| Opening Entries | Till sessions |
| Item Group, Item Groups | Category, Categories |
| UOM | Unit |
| Nos | piece |
| Item Code | Product code (filled in for you) |
| Customer Credit | Owing |
| Posting Date | Date |
| Apply Discount On | Discount |
| Credit Card | Card |
| Bank Draft, Cheque, Wire Transfer | Other |
| Charge (the till button) | Pay |
| The long invoice number on a receipt | A short receipt number such as #0045, with the full reference printed under it |
| MPESA | M-Pesa |
| POS Profile | Till settings |
| Staff | Team (under Settings) |

Some words are no longer shown at all, because shops do not need them: Customer Group, Territory, All Warehouses, Work In
Progress, Finished Goods, Goods In Transit, and Stock Entry (unless the shop makes goods).

## Where things went

| To do this | Go to |
|---|---|
| Take a sale | Go to till (top of the screen) |
| See sales, unpaid sales, returns, or till sessions | Sales, then the tab you want |
| See who owes you | Customers, Owing; or Home |
| Add or change a product | Products |
| Count stock, see low stock or stock that expires | Stock levels, then the tab you want |
| Order from a supplier or receive goods | Purchases |
| Pay a supplier bill | Purchases, open the bill, Pay |
| Record money you spent | Expenses, New expense |
| See profit, sales or money owed | Reports, or Home |
| Change the shop name, tax, till options or team | Settings |
| Switch the New look on or off | Settings, New look |

Some jobs still open the earlier screen from a link, such as the bulk price update, variations, importing products, locations and
stock transfers, recipes and wastage, and the keys for M-Pesa and other payment gateways. They work as before.

## The page buttons

Each page now shows its main button and a **More** menu. Export, Import and the less common jobs are in More. On Products, More
also holds Update many prices, and with a second store Stores, Stock transfers and Move stock between stores.

## If you cannot find something

Use the search box at the top, or ask the manager. If the New look gets in the way of selling, the manager can turn it off in
Settings and the old screens come back at once. Nothing is lost: sales, stock and money records stay as they are.

## Before the manager switches back

Finish or clear held sales on every till. Carts and held sales stay on the device they were made on.
